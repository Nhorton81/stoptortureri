  • ​Breaking: Justice For Jerry Cintron, August 2025

    • Jerry Cintron won his case in the First Circuit federal court after a federal judge ruled that Rhode Island prisons officials can be held personally liable for locking someone in solitary confinement while they visibly deteriorate - mentally and physically - which happened to Jerry Cintron over 450 days in solitary confinement.

  • The Stop Torture RI Coalition, DARE, SURJ and other conducted a Car Rally & Press Conference to Spread Awareness about the Constant Lock Downs in the ACI, September 2024

We stand with those enduring grave injustices—including the deprivation of education, rehabilitation, and recreation—as a result of drastically reduced out-of-cell time.​

  • The Stop Torture RI Coalition & DARE held a Vigil and Acting Against Medical Neglect in RI Prisons at the RI Statehouse, March 2024

    • Join us for a vigil honoring Willie Washington Jr. and the countless others who have died from neglect inside the ACI. We will gather at the Rhode Island State House on Thursday, March 14th at 3:00 PM to support Willie’s family and acknowledge the lives taken by medical neglect and solitary confinement abuses.​

  • The Stop Torture RI Coalition & The Wheeler Middle School Collaborated to Raised Funds to Help Reform Solitary Confinement, February 2023

Your financial support is essential to making this work possible. Join us on April 20, 2024, from 12–3 PM at the Providence Public Library.

  • The Behind The Walls Committee & The Stop Torture RI Coalition conducted a protest at the PVD Fest where they carried 7 Caskets to represent the 7 Deaths in the ACI, September 2023

  • Seven individuals have died in Rhode Island’s prisons this year, including two in the past month and four by suicide. Yet not a single independent investigation has taken place. Without external accountability, RIDOC and RIBCO are able to continue business as usual while lives are being lost.

  • RI Correctional Officers Provoked a Peaceful Protest Due to Their Dislike of the New Solitary Policies, August 2023

  • There was a disturbance at the ACI last night, and more than 30 SWAT officers entered the prison with guns and shields. Yet, correctional officers deny this happened despite the pictures.

  • The Federal Judge Passed Litigation Limiting the Usage of Solitary Confinement to 30 Maximum, July 2023 

    • Individuals can be held in solitary confinement for no more than 30 days, with a guaranteed minimum of 2 hours out-of-cell time daily, seven days a week.

    • Infractions have been reorganized into four disciplinary classes:

      • Class 3 and 4: Reprimand only

      • Class 2: Up to 15 days in solitary confinement, 2 hours out of cell time

      • Class 1: Up to 30 days in solitary confinement, 3 hours out of cell time after 15 days in solitary confinement

    • Disciplinary hearings are no longer conducted by DOC Lieutenants or Captains but by an independent hearing officer with experience in probation or parole, ensuring a more impartial process.

  • After 7 deaths in the ACI, 4 of them were suicides while in Solitary Confinement the Stop Torture RI Coalition conducted a Rally for Brian Rodenas and Other Victims of Suicide at the ACI, May 2023

    • Brian Rodenas spent months in solitary confinement at the ACI. Even as he showed clear signs of crisis and begged for help, staff failed to respond—until it was too late. Last week, Brian died. The DOC has called it a suicide, but his family believes the circumstances are suspicious and is demanding a full investigation.

    • Brian is now the third person to lose their life at the ACI in the past three months.

    • This cannot continue. We must act. Please join us Tuesday at 2:45 PM outside the State House to call for immediate passage of the Reform Solitary Confinement Act.​
